# Code of conduct

Oikonome is a small project about people's money, and the conversations
around it are held to the standard that implies.

**Expected.** Be direct and be kind. Assume the other person is competent
and acting in good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person. Keep
reports reproducible and requests specific; keep replies to the point.

**Not accepted.** Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind;
sexualised language or attention; publishing someone else's private
information — and in this project that expressly includes any financial
detail a screenshot, log, or issue reveals about anyone; sustained
disruption of a discussion after being asked to stop.

**Scope.** Everything under the `oikonome` organisation on GitHub — issues,
pull requests, discussions, commits — and any place where you are
representing the project.

**Enforcement.** Report a problem to support@oikonome.com. Reports are read
by the maintainer, handled privately, and never shared with the person
reported without the reporter's agreement. Anyone who breaks this code may
have their comment removed, their contribution declined, or their access to
the project's spaces withdrawn, depending on what happened.
